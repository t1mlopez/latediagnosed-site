I Don't Know Where to Start

If you've just discovered this about yourself — breathe.

What you're feeling right now is valid. The grief, the relief, the anger, the confusion — all of it. A late diagnosis doesn't erase your past, but it can illuminate a path forward.

You are not alone. Thousands of adults discover their neurodivergence every day. We're here to help you figure out what comes next.

Our Mission

Late Diagnosed exists to provide evidence-based resources, peer support, and community for adults who discover their neurodivergence later in life. We believe understanding yourself is a right — not a privilege.
